1. Scope and Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how VERIXLABS ("VERIXLABS," "we," "us," or "our") handles personal information when you use LeanMindset, our related websites, support channels, and services (collectively, the "Service").

For questions or privacy requests, contact our Privacy Contact at hello@leanmindset.app or hello@leanmindset.app.

2. Information We May Collect

2.1 Account and Profile Information

Name, username, email address, phone number if used, authentication identifiers, and account settings.

Age or date-of-birth range where needed for eligibility, safety, or personalization.

Profile information you choose to provide, such as gender, height, weight, goals, activity level, food preferences, dietary preferences, fitness experience, and similar wellness information.

2.2 Health, Fitness and Wellness Information

Depending on the features you use, you may provide information that can be sensitive, including weight history, body measurements, nutrition logs, calorie or macro information, exercise and activity records, sleep or recovery information, goals, progress entries, photos, wellness responses, and information you choose to share with a coach or support team.

If LeanMindset integrates with Apple Health/HealthKit, Health Connect, wearables, or similar platforms, we may receive only the data types you authorize and only for enabled features. The exact permissions requested should be disclosed at the time of connection.

2.3 Meal, Exercise and App Activity Data

Meals, foods, grocery items, recipes, nutrition preferences, exercise selections, completed workouts, habits, goals, check-ins, and progress history.

Feature usage, screen interactions, timestamps, crash information, device type, operating-system version, app version, and diagnostic information.

Approximate region or locale derived from device settings or network information where needed for language, units, legal notices, fraud prevention, or service operation.

2.4 Communications and Support

When you contact us, we may collect the content of your request, attachments you provide, contact details, and information needed to investigate or resolve the issue. If coaching or messaging features are available, communications may be processed and stored to provide those features, enforce safety rules, and address complaints.

2.5 Payment and Subscription Information

For purchases through Apple or Google, payment-card information is generally processed by the applicable app store and is not provided to us in full. We may receive subscription status, product identifier, purchase or renewal status, transaction identifiers, country/region, and related entitlement information. If we later offer direct payments, the payment processor and relevant data handling must be added to this Policy.

3. How We Use Personal Information

Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure your account.

Provide personalized fitness, nutrition, habit, coaching, and progress-tracking features.

Calculate or estimate goals, calories, activity, trends, progress, and recommendations.

Operate subscriptions and determine feature entitlements.

Provide support, respond to requests, investigate technical problems, and communicate important service notices.

Protect users, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines, and maintain service security.

Debug, analyze, maintain, and improve product reliability and usability.

Comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Send marketing communications only where permitted and with any consent required by law.

4. Automated Recommendations and AI

LeanMindset may use automated systems or artificial intelligence to generate or help generate suggestions, summaries, meal ideas, exercise plans, behavioral prompts, or other personalized content. Inputs may include information you provide and information generated through your use of LeanMindset. Automated outputs may be inaccurate and are not medical advice.

We will not represent automated recommendations as professional medical diagnosis or treatment. If a third-party AI provider processes user data, that provider and the categories of data sent must be identified before publication of this Policy.

5. Consent and Your Choices

Where consent is the appropriate legal basis, we seek consent that is appropriate to the sensitivity of the information and the context. You may withdraw consent for optional processing, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawing certain permissions may disable related features.

Health-platform permissions can generally be changed in your device or platform settings.

Push notifications can be changed in device settings.

Marketing emails can be unsubscribed from using the link provided in the message, where applicable.

Optional profile fields may be edited or removed through available account controls.

You may request access, correction, or deletion as described below.

Apple, Google, payment platforms, health platforms, wearables, or other services you choose to connect.

Coaches or service providers where you request or enable a coaching/service relationship and the information is needed to provide that service.

Professional advisers, auditors, insurers, or transaction counterparties subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

Law enforcement, regulators, courts, or other parties where disclosure is required or permitted by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to provide the Service, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, protect security, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data category.

Active account/profile data: generally retained while the account remains active.

User-generated wellness history: retained while needed to provide history/progress features unless deleted earlier by the user or account deletion process.

Support and safety records: retained for a period reasonably necessary to resolve issues, prevent abuse, and meet legal requirements.

Transaction and subscription records: retained as required for accounting, fraud prevention, tax, and legal obligations.

Backups: deleted or overwritten on a rolling schedule consistent with our backup architecture, subject to legal holds and technical limitations.

10. Account and Data Deletion

If you have a LeanMindset account, you may request account deletion using the in-app deletion path or the web/contact method we provide.
